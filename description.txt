This is the third iteration of a mod originally meant to just do things with reforges, but I got so caught up with Summoner, that I decided to add a bit more for them

The mod comes with three new reforge stats for weapons...
- Summon Cost, increases/decreases how many slots minions/sentries occupy
- Summon Speed, increases/decreases the speed of minions/sentries
- Armor Penetration, for summoner, which scales off of the enemy's Defense

As well as new accessory reforges, such as:
- 1% and 3% versions of the Crit Chance reforges
- Arcane is reworked to give mana regeneration, along with being given versions for different tiers
- Added accessory reforges that increase Ranged Velocity and Ammo Conservation chance

The mod also introduces Tinkerer Hammers, a new type of item that is necessary for reforging items. However, they provide additional stats and effects to them as well!
Tinkerer hammers each have a Tier, which corresponds to the power level of the reforges you can get. Every reforge has been reworked and sorted into tiers, within within which all reforges are meant to have the same power level!
Negative reforges have also been removed and reworked into low tier positive ones!

Aside from that, the mod also makes it so the last 3 reforges cannot be rolled again, which, compounded with the above changes, should make getting a desirable reforge much easier.
