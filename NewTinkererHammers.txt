
Terminology:
	Summoner Weapon: Anything that deals Summon Damage
	Summon: Either a Minion or a Sentry. Not a whip.
	Summon Weapons: Minion or Sentry summoning staves
	Power of Five: "If 5 accessories are reforged with this hammer" (NOTE: I would underline this if I could, since it's a keyword)
	Power of Three: "If 3 accessories are reforged with this hammer"
	. . . You get the point

Summon Stat Ideas:
	- Summon Speed
	- Summon Tag Effectiveness
	- Armor Penetration

Hammers now have tiers, specifying the tier of reforges they can apply

Tier system:
Tier 1: Randomly found + Early Hardmode, until Evil Boss
Tier 2: From evil boss (craftable with tissue samples, so you can get it before if you suck) onward + Queen Bee
Tier 3: First Mech boss onward
Tier 4: Golem onward

NOTE: Fuck Calamity, if Calamity is installed, the rest of the Hardmode ore hammers are craftable from Hardmode Fishing Crates instead (NOTE: Check shimmer after)
