
Terminology:
	Summoner Weapon: Anything that deals Summon Damage
	Summon: Either a Minion or a Sentry. Not a whip.
	Summon Weapons: Minion or Sentry summoning staves
	Power of Five: "If 5 accessories are reforged with this hammer" (NOTE: I would underline this if I could, since it's a keyword)
	Power of Three: "If 3 accessories are reforged with this hammer"
	. . . You get the point

Summon Stat Ideas:
	- Summon Speed
	- Summon Tag Effectiveness
	- Armor Penetration

Hammers now have tiers, specifying the tier of reforges they can apply

Tier system:
Tier 1: Randomly found + Early Hardmode, until Evil Boss
Tier 2: From evil boss (craftable with tissue samples, so you can get it before if you suck) onward + Queen Bee
Tier 3: First Mech boss onward
Tier 4: Golem onward

Tier 1 Hammers:
	Copper: +1 Defense
	Tin: +1% Movement and Jump Speed
	Iron: +5% Damage to Weapons
	Lead: +5% Crit Chance to Weapons
	Silver: +3% Ranged Velocity on accessories, Ranged weapons get +5 Armor Penetration
	Tungsten: -2% Mana Cost on accessories, +8% casting speed on magic weapons
	Gold: 2% Melee Speed on accessories, Melee weapons and whips get +16% size
	Platinum: Power of Five: +1 Minion Slot. +6% Summon Speed for Summon Weapons
	Jungle: Weapons inflict Poison on hit
	Ice: Power of Four: All attacks inflict Frostburn.
	Fossil: +4% Mining Speed

Tier 2 Hammers:
	Honeycomb: Attacking with this weapon periodically summons additional bees. (NOTE: Make bees and giant bees use STATIC immunity! Global immunity should be illegal!)
	Meteorite: This weapon deals 7% more damage and has its damage variance tripled!
	Demonite: +2% Movement Speed and Acceleration
	Crimtane: +0.5HP/s regeneration if you haven't been hit for 15 seconds
	Obsidian: +1% Damage Reduction
	Hellstone: Attacking with this weapon periodically summons an explosion, dealing the hit's damage class and applying Hellfire for a short time
	Eternian: +5% classless damage

	Gelatinous: Power of Three: Crystal Assassin dash on a cooldown. Weapons get +30% Knockback
	Cobalt: Power of Five: Cobalt Shield, but -2 Defense
	Palladium: Hitting enemies with this weapon gives you +1.5 HP/s regeneration for 2 seconds.
	Mythril: Increase damage by up to 20% based on the enemy's remaining health
	Orichalcum: Power of Five: Panic Necklace
	Adamantite: This weapon's projectiles can penetrate 50% more targets (rounded down), and lose less damage from penetrating enemies.
	Titanium: Power of Five: Hermes Boots and +3% Movement Speed
